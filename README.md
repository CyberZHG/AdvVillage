# Adventurer Village

English | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

**Play:** https://cyberzhg.github.io/AdvVillage/

A pixel-art village builder in the spirit of Kairosoft games. You lay roads and raise buildings; adventurers, gatherers, villagers and visitors live their lives on their own. The village keeps running even when you leave it idle.

- **Build** houses, shops, restaurants, taverns, a spa, a theater, an arena and more. Every door faces a road, and certain buildings placed near each other form combos that boost income and appeal. Decorations such as lamps, benches, ponds, a windmill and a clock tower lift the takings of nearby businesses.
- **Specialty goods**: shops turn monster materials and gathered finds into specialties (honey cakes, herb tea, pearl souvenirs) that sell at a premium.
- **Adventurers** in eight classes hunt monsters by themselves, take subjugation requests, buy better gear, forge their weapons, armor and accessories, earn titles, find rivals, form partnerships and sometimes marry and raise the next generation. Follow any of them with the camera and read their personal story.
- **100 monsters** roam the wilds around each village or wait at ten expedition sites, with rare shining variants and bosses that the whole guild rallies against.
- **Visitors** sightsee and spend money; regulars grow familiar with the village and send gifts.
- **Seasonal commissions** set a few goals each season, and the village's **fame** grows into a reputation that draws food critics, foreign caravans, royal visits, master smiths, sages and wandering heroes.
- **Seasons, weather and festivals** change the scenery and the crowds. Random events and travelling merchants keep things moving without ever pausing the game.
- **Evaluation** after 16 years unlocks the next of five villages, each with its own land and challenges. Play continues afterwards.
- Achievements, a bestiary, a yearly village gazette, shareable images, and save import/export.
- Available in English, 中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
