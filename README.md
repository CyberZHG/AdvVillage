# Adventurer Village

English | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

**Play:** https://cyberzhg.github.io/AdvVillage/

A pixel-art village builder in the spirit of Kairosoft games. You lay roads and raise buildings; adventurers, gatherers, villagers and visitors live their lives on their own. The village keeps running even when you leave it idle.

- **Build** houses, shops, restaurants, taverns, a spa, a theater, an arena and more. Every door faces a road, and certain buildings placed near each other form combos that boost income and appeal. Decorations such as lamps, benches, ponds, a windmill and a clock tower lift the takings of nearby businesses.
- **Specialty goods**: shops turn monster materials and gathered finds into specialties (honey cakes, herb tea, pearl souvenirs) that sell at a premium.
- **Adventurers** in eight classes hunt monsters by themselves, take subjugation requests, buy better gear, forge their weapons, armor and accessories, earn titles, advance to higher classes at Lv.100, find rivals, form partnerships and sometimes marry and raise the next generation. Follow any of them with the camera and read their personal story.
- **100 monsters** roam the wilds around each village or wait at ten expedition sites, with rare shining variants and bosses that the whole guild rallies against. The wilds grow stronger alongside the village, expedition sites open ever deeper floors, and now and then a defeated monster follows the party home as a companion.
- **Mochi, the guild cat**, arrives in one of nine coats, naps on doorsteps, follows villagers around and grows fond of the village, bringing small presents and luck to the shop it sits at.
- **Research** turns gold and materials into lasting improvements, with a short queue and optional automation for research, forging and upgrades.
- **The wilds** are dotted with wildflowers, stumps, fallen logs, ruins and crystals. Trees and rocks rise out of the ground as they grow back, and monsters walk in from the edge of the map toward the village.
- **Skills** grow on their own as adventurers fight: four shared skills plus one per class, such as Cleave, Shield Block, Critical Strike and Evasion.
- **Seasons** change the land: golden grass in autumn, snow in winter, and certain shops draw bigger crowds in their season. Festivals hang pennants on the eaves and bring crowds into the streets.
- **Visitors** sightsee and spend money; regulars grow familiar with the village and send gifts.
- **Seasonal commissions** set a few goals each season, and the village's **fame** grows into a reputation that draws food critics, foreign caravans, royal visits, master smiths, sages and wandering heroes.
- **Seasons, weather and festivals** change the scenery and the crowds. Random events and travelling merchants keep things moving without ever pausing the game.
- **Evaluation** after 16 years unlocks the next of five villages, each with its own land and challenges. Play continues afterwards.
- Time away is settled when you return, and research keeps going while you are gone. Achievements, a bestiary, monthly charts, a yearly village gazette, shareable images, save import/export, and offline play as an installable app.
- Available in English, 中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
