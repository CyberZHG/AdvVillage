# Adventurer Village

English | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

**Play:** https://cyberzhg.github.io/AdvVillage/

A pixel-art village builder game. You lay roads and raise buildings; adventurers, gatherers, villagers and visitors live their lives on their own, and the village keeps running while you leave it idle.

- Build shops, inns, workshops and wonders; adventurers hunt monsters, take requests, go on expeditions and grow stronger.
- Five villages, each with its own land, climate and story. After 16 years the village is scored and the next one unlocks; play carries on afterwards.
